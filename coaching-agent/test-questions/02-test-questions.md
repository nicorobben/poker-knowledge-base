# Doc 02 — Acceptance-test questions

> Five questions the coaching agent must answer correctly using only Doc 02 + Doc 1. Each question targets a specific section of Doc 02 and tests whether the agent has internalized both the modern baseline and the Doc 1 framing it builds on.

---

### Q1. "Will Ma's MIT course says UTG opens about 6% of hands at 9-max. Every modern chart I see shows UTG opening closer to 13%. Is one of them wrong?"

*(Tests §1 "conditional baseline" principle, §10 comparison table, and honest handling of Doc 1 per Standing Rule #6.)*

---

### Q2. "I'm in the big blind at a 100bb online cash table. The button opens to 2.5bb. What's my defense frequency, and how does it split between call and 3-bet?"

*(Tests §7 BB defense — the single most commonly misunderstood frequency in modern poker. Correct answer must give ~44% continuance with the call/3-bet/fold split, and must explain why 44% is specific to vs. BTN rather than a universal number.)*

---

### Q3. "I have 12 big blinds on the button in an MTT. Folded to me. Should I open-raise or open-shove?"

*(Tests §4 push-fold framework and the 15bb / 10bb boundary. Tests whether the agent correctly situates 12bb between the two canonical benchmarks — closer to raise-first-in territory than pure push-fold — and resists the student's instinct to collapse to shove-only too early.)*

---

### Q4. "The CO opens to 2.25bb. I'm on the button. Should my 3-bet range be polarized with small suited aces as bluffs, or linear/merged with top-of-range hands?"

*(Tests §5 3-bet construction — the most visible place where Doc 1's 2016 polarization teaching has been specialized by modern solver work. The correct answer is linear/merged IP vs. a late open, with explicit framing of where this differs from and where it agrees with Doc 1's L4 treatment.)*

---

### Q5. "I'm a cash-game player who also plays MTTs. For MTTs with antes, do I just use the same opening ranges I use in cash, or do I need to do something different?"

*(Tests §9 antes adjustments plus §3 stack-depth gradient. Correct answer covers the ~4–6% range widening from antes, the sizing bump, the stack-depth question the student didn't ask about but needs to answer to give useful advice, and the chipEV vs. ICM separation that forwards to Doc 04.)*
