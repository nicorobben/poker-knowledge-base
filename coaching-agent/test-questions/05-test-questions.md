# Doc 05 — Acceptance-test questions

> Five questions the coaching agent must answer correctly using only Doc 05 + Doc 1. Each question targets a specific section of Doc 05 and tests whether the agent has internalized both the review methodology and the Doc 1 framing it builds on.

---

### Q1. "I played a 4-hour session last night. Do I just go through every hand I played and see what went wrong?"

*(Tests §3 hand-selection filter and §1 review philosophy. Tests whether the agent resists the student's instinct to review every hand — the single most common beginner mistake — and instead teaches the "interesting hand" filter. Correct answer must give the concrete filter criteria and explain why reviewing every hand is actively counterproductive.)*

---

### Q2. "When should I actually sit down to review — right after the session while it's fresh, or the next day?"

*(Tests §2 review timing. Tests whether the agent knows the modern answer is "next day" for the deep review and can explain the cognitive-consolidation reason, while also distinguishing it from the short post-session journaling pass that should happen immediately. Must separate the two review moments rather than collapsing them.)*

---

### Q3. "I found a hand I misplayed — I called down with ace-high on a board where I clearly should have folded. Now what? Just move on and play better next time?"

*(Tests §6 leak-to-drill conversion — the "review must close" mechanism and the single most common gap between students who improve and students who don't. Correct answer must reject "play better next time" as the failure mode, name what a drill actually is, and walk through the conversion using the conversion table or equivalent structure.)*

---

### Q4. "I'm going through my session and one hand I'm sure I played wrong — I open-raised KJo from UTG at 9-max and got 3-bet and folded. Is this a preflop leak, a postflop leak, or what?"

*(Tests §5 leak categorization framework — the five buckets — and the discipline of categorizing correctly rather than lumping everything under "I played badly." Correct answer must classify this as preflop range construction, note that the assumption the open was wrong is itself conditional (see Doc 02), and walk the student through the Doc 1 L1 pot-odds framing plus the Doc 05 five-bucket taxonomy.)*

---

### Q5. "Doc 1 gives a 7-step workflow for analyzing a hand. Why does Doc 05 have a different 7-step workflow? Which one do I use?"

*(Tests §4 session review workflow — the extension-not-replacement framing per Standing Rule #6 — and the honest handling of where modern review methodology builds on top of Doc 1 rather than contradicting it. Correct answer must frame the two workflows as nested (Doc 1's steps live inside Doc 05's step 4 — "analyze the hand"), not competing, and explain what the session-level scaffolding (selection, prioritization, categorization, drill conversion) adds that the single-hand workflow doesn't cover.)*
